R&C Privacy Policy

App: R&C (Reading & Comprehension) · Developer: WANAKU LLC
Effective date: February 15, 2026· Last updated: November 20, 2026

Summary

R&C does not collect, sell or share personal information. There are no advertisements, no analytics or tracking tools, and no accounts stored on our servers. Everything you create in the app, such as progress, points and classrooms, stays on your own device.

Information stored on your device

To work properly, the app saves the following locally on your device only:

  • Learning progress: completed lessons, points (coins), level, and your language choice.
  • Whether you have finished the onboarding screens.
  • For teachers: a teacher profile, classrooms, student names or nicknames entered by the teacher, assignments, and completion records.

This information is not sent to WANAKU LLC or to any third party by the app. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Camera

The app asks for camera permission only to scan QR codes, for example to join a class or to record that a student completed an assignment. Camera images are processed on your device to read the code. They are not saved, uploaded or shared.

Read-aloud (text-to-speech)

The read-aloud feature uses the speech engine built into your device (for example Google Text-to-Speech on Android or Apple's speech engine on iOS). That engine is provided and governed by your device's operating system, not by us.

Lesson content updates

All app updates through Google Play.

Sentence and AI feedback

Answers and sentences are checked on your device and are not transmitted.

Children

R&C is an educational app that includes content for young learners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because the app does not collect or transmit personal information. Teachers and parents who enter a child's name or nickname into a classroom should know that it is stored only on the teacher's device.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties because we do not receive it. The app is distributed through Google Play and the Apple App Store, which handle downloads and updates under their own privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

Because data lives only on your device, you control it. To delete everything, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your device settings. Inside the app, the reset-progress option clears learning progress.

Security

Data is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Anyone with access to an unlocked device may be able to open the app, so please protect your device with a screen lock.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change materially affects how information is handled, we will also note it in the app's store listing or release notes.
